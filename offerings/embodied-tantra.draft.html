A Lived, Embodied Path

A two-day immersive initiation into Tantra as a lived, embodied path, followed by a Tantrik Temple designed to integrate, ground, and close the journey in ritual and truth. This is where intimacy becomes conscious. Where eros becomes sacred. Where the body becomes a doorway to truth.

Held at The Conscious Place in Amsterdam. You're here because deep down you know there is more. More to intimacy than you've yet to taste. More depth than you've let yourself explore.

Before You Book

Who This Is For, and the Format

Beginner-friendly, and deep enough for those with years of tantra behind them. Solo attendance is the norm. You do not need a partner to come, and if you have one, they do not need to come with you.

  • Held in a group container (for private sessions, see Couples Work)
  • Two full days of training, followed by an evening Deeper Intimacy Temple
  • A mixed group: solo, coupled, open relationships, ENM, all held equally
  • Deeper Intimacy is an advanced, applied-to space: this Weekend is how first-timers qualify to enter it
  • Co-facilitated in partnership with Temple of Radiance
  • Consent-led throughout, with every practice offered as an invitation
  • Nervous beforehand is normal. Almost everyone is.
