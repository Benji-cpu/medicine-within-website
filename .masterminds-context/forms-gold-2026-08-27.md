## 1. THE ACQUISITION FINDING (153 answers to "how did you hear")

| Channel | Count | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Hipsy | 56 | 36% |
| Friend / word of mouth | 38 | 24% |
| Unclear | 26 | 16% |
| Instagram | 15 | 9% |
| WhatsApp group | 11 | 7% |
| Email / newsletter | 4 | 2% |
| Knew Sandi already | 3 | 1% |

**Hipsy's marketplace plus word of mouth is 60% of everyone who fills a form.
Instagram is 9%. The newsletter is 2%.**

This is the single most expensive misallocation in the business. The content
effort goes to Instagram; the buyers arrive from a ticketing marketplace and from
each other. Two consequences worth acting on:
- Hipsy listing copy, images and the events widget are higher-leverage than a reel.
- Word of mouth is 24% and completely unincentivised. There is no referral offer.

## 2. THE #1 WORD IS "MYSELF"

Across every intention and excitement answer:
myself (57) · connection (52) · people (49) · connect (42) · temple (42) ·
explore (40) · space (36) · experience (33) · others (32) · intimacy (32) ·
energy (29) · open (25) · tantra (24) · flow (22) · sensuality (21) · body (21)

Refines the earlier "people is #1" finding: on *want* questions specifically,
**"myself" edges out "people."** They come back to themselves, by way of other
people. Tantra (24) and sensuality (21) rank low. **They are not buying tantra.
They are buying a room where they get to be themselves in front of others.**

## 3. THEY ALREADY THINK THEY ARE GOOD AT CONSENT (207 self-scores)

Mean 8.3 out of 10. **83% score themselves 8 or higher.** Only 10 people scored 6
or below.

Do not sell "learn consent and boundaries." They believe that box is ticked.
Sell what becomes possible *after* it. This also carries a facilitation risk worth
naming: a room of self-declared 8s is a room with blind spots.

## 4. THE PAIN DATA, finally (25 answers, ET forms only)

The question "what have you tried that didn't work" exists on only two forms and
**about a third answered "nothing, everything worked."** What did surface:

**Goal-orientation, named repeatedly and unprompted:**
- "To take it more easy, not have sex as the goal (somehow it still always becomes the goal) and to reduce the pressure."
- "I try to enjoy intimacy more without having a goal in mind. Sometimes that works, but not always."
- "lower expectations of what needs to happen and feel sensations without them necessarily leading to an orgasm."

**One-sided initiation, the loudest emotional line in the whole corpus:**
- "Everything works, but it always have to come from me and I would like to reverse/change this pattern! I think we are way bigger and better than this, like we are in every other aspect of life already!"

**⭐ THE COMMERCIAL LINE OF THE ENTIRE PASS:**
> **"We do not do anything with what we learned in previous workshops."**

That is the Living Temple's reason to exist, written by a customer, unprompted,
before the Living Temple was designed. Use it verbatim.

## 5. UNADDRESSED PRE-EVENT OBJECTIONS (from 100 "anything to know" answers)

**Body shame and dress code, raised unprompted and repeatedly:**
- "I gained more weight last year and that is just a thing in my mind. I find it hard to show my body, and at the same time that feels so sad. I am curious what the dress code is :) I will dare my mind and embrace my body."
- "I am not really sure what to wear, as I don't own any temple clothes. Last time I ended up wearing nothing. And that time before I just wore white shorts."

**Group-size fear:**
- "I know myself to be a little frightened of large groups yet still always looking for experiences in groups to challenge a few of my beliefs"
- "Usually I am shy with bigger groups in the beginning, but I always lose it. I just need a bit of time."
- "I am a bit nervous but also very excited, I might be shy at first."

**Heartbreak and recovery as the actual state on arrival:**
- "Still healing my heart after a break up."
- "Recovering from stressful period, taking it slow and easy."
- "I'm a bit tired right now because I have some really busy weeks behind me, but I will take responsibility for myself when needed."

None of this is answered anywhere on the site or in the pre-event emails. A dress
code line and a "you can arrive tired" line would remove real friction.

2. **Build a referral ask.** Word of mouth is 24% of discovery and earns nothing.
