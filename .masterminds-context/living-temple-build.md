## THE HEADLINE SHIFT
The Living Temple was designed as a **3-month quarterly cohort** tied to ET+DI.
Two independent inputs now say it should be the **ongoing monthly spine of the
business** instead.

### Input 1 — the sales data
- 414 paying buyers. 124 bought more than once.
- Those 124 = 30% of buyers producing **54% of all revenue** (EUR 38,293 of 71,413).
- Repeat buyer LTV EUR 309. One-timer EUR 114. Nearly 3x.
- Of ~156 second purchases, **110 were the same offer again.** Temple to Temple 47
  times. Temple to Retreat: once, in five years.
- Conclusion: people do not climb a ladder. They come back to the room. This is a
  repeat-attendance community business, and the Living Temple is the product that
  actually matches it.
- Available population right now: 124 repeat buyers, 36 with 2+ paid temple nights,
  26 of those men.

### Input 2 — Joe's market research (2026-08-20 VN)
He asked three women directly about the Living Temple idea. What came back:
1. **"There's never really any good looking guys there. No one I want to connect
   with."** Who is in the room is the buying decision.
2. **"I hate it when guys WhatsApp me. We didn't connect after the temple."**
   Creepy no-context DMs, and being added to WhatsApp groups afterwards.
3. **"It depends who's in it. If it was people they'd be excited to connect with,
   yes. If it was just randos, no."**
4. His warning: if women do not feel safe in the Living Temple, the whole temple
   falls apart.

**This makes curation and safety the product, not a feature.** The page cannot be
"buy a seat, EUR 333." It has to be applied-for and visibly protected.

## PRICE, SETTLED 2026-08-27 (with two loose ends)

Confirmed against Sandi's own pricing sheet, formulas live and hand-checked:
`docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/12va1sBKOzFPbLnpf5J__3U8aAjFtSO4p_H6SIPrDLX4`

**Founding: EUR 69/month, or EUR 169 prepaid for the opening three months.**
**Standard: EUR 95/month once the founding places are gone.**

Monthly at EUR 69:
| Members | Per month | Per year |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 345 | 4,140 |
| 10 | 690 | 8,280 |
| 15 | 1,035 | 12,420 |
| 20 | 1,380 | 16,560 |
| 30 | 2,070 | 24,840 |

Prepay at EUR 169/quarter: 5 = 845, 10 = 1,690, 15 = 2,535, 20 = 3,380, 30 = 5,070
per quarter. At 30 members that is EUR 20,280/year, so EUR 4,560/year less than
everyone staying monthly. The prepay saving is 18.4% (EUR 207 becomes EUR 169).
Sheet also carries a Scenario C at a realistic 50/50 monthly/prepay split.

### ⚠ Loose end 1: no prepay price for the standard tier
EUR 169 against EUR 95/month (EUR 285 for three) is a 41% discount, which is
almost certainly not intended. Either set a second prepay number for standard, or
make prepay explicitly a founding-entry offer only.

### ⚠ Loose end 2: the old cohort price has not been retired
`living_temple_rhythm` still carries EUR 333 founding / EUR 555 full for a 3-month
cohort. That is the same room at a very different price. One of the two has to be
formally killed or they collide in the funnel. **Sandi has not confirmed which.**

### Recommended resolution (not yet approved)
Treat EUR 169 as the founding entry that covers the opening three months, after
which membership rolls at EUR 69/month. That way prepay is a door, not a permanent
parallel track, which removes the EUR 4,560 leak and the 41% standard-tier problem
in one move. Retire EUR 333 / EUR 555 entirely.
