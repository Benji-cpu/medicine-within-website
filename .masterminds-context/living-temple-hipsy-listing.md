## EVENT DESCRIPTION (paste as-is)

The temple closes in the evening. The work doesn't. 🕯️

You know the drop. Sunday morning after temple, something in you is still open, still humming, still closer to yourself than you have been in weeks. And there is nowhere to put it. So you go back to work, you answer the messages, and by Wednesday it has become a nice memory of a night you had, instead of a change in how you live.

Or it goes the other way. Something old surfaced and did not close again, and you carried it alone into a week with no room for it.

Both are real. Both are the work. 🙏

And you know the high. The days after, when a few of you keep messaging because nobody wants it to end. I know that one from the inside. I have walked out of a night so expansive I could barely land, my field full of people and tribe and vibe, and come home to my own flat and felt the loneliness arrive. Nothing had gone wrong. For one night I had the thing I actually want.

Longing like that is holy. It is the body remembering what it is capable of. It is asking to be answered, and one night a month cannot answer it. 🌹

⟡

🌙 The Living Temple™ is the room between the nights.

A membership. Part online, part in a room together. The place you belong to between the temple nights, where what opens on a Saturday gets to keep moving through your ordinary week. Where you meet yourself, others, and Life more fully, more deeply, and then bring that home with you.

The temple crowd. The ones you recognize across a candlelit room. People who came for the night and stayed for the people. And it deepens, because the people stay. You learn each other's stories. You notice when someone goes quiet, and they notice you. That is what a community is. 💛

Every month

Time to deepen into connection with the people you keep meeting on the temple floor. At the temple house it is free and the cacao is on me. When we go out, you cover your own.
