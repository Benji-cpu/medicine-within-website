## The three prompts, saved for reuse

### Prompt 1 · Tighten the offer

> Use the Branding Agent / Branding Manager to help me tighten my offer before I improve my brand and funnel.
>
> Offer page URL: [PASTE URL]
> If the URL is blank, ask me to type or paste my offer.
>
> Then:
> 1. Summarize the offer in plain English.
> 2. Tell me who it is really for.
> 3. Tell me what result it promises.
> 4. Tell me what feels vague, generic, weak, or confusing.
> 5. Suggest a tighter version of: the offer statement, the audience statement, the promise, the differentiator.
> 6. Tell me what proof would make this easier to buy.
> 7. Give me 3 stronger positioning options, from safest to boldest.
> 8. If my Speak Human profile is available, run the final user-facing language through it before you show me the result.
>
> Important: do not hallucinate services or outcomes. If anything is unclear, ask a short follow-up question instead of guessing. Optimize for clarity and conversion, not cleverness.

### Prompt 2 · Build a first-pass brand brain

> Using the Branding Agent, build a first-pass brand brain for this business.
>
> Rules:
> 1. Ask me for my website if you do not already have it.
> 2. If I do not have a website, let me type or paste the offer instead.
> 3. Read the website or offer carefully before writing anything.
> 4. Use the frameworks in the Branding Agent repo when useful, especially: awareness levels, competitive alternatives, mental vs visible market, value equation, jobs to be done, common enemy.
> 5. Fill out a practical brand brain, not a fluffy one.
> 6. Keep your wording concrete enough that I could reuse it in copy, content, and sales.
> 7. Flag anything that is vague, generic, or unsupported.
> 8. End with: the one-sentence positioning statement, the strongest promise, the likely audience awareness level, the biggest messaging gap, the 3 biggest open questions.
> 9. If my Speak Human profile is available, run the final user-facing language through it before you show me the result.
>
> If needed, use templates/brand-brain-template.md.

### Prompt 3 · Evaluate the page against the ICP

> Using the Branding Manager, evaluate my website or this page: [PASTE THE URL YOU WANT TO EVALUATE HERE]
>
> Please do all of the following:
> 1. Evaluate the page from the perspective of my ICP.
> 2. Tell me whether the branding and messaging feel aligned with the right buyer.
> 3. Tell me what feels unclear, generic, off-brand, weak, or mismatched.
> 4. Suggest changes to the branding on the landing page that would better fit my ICP.
> 5. Suggest changes to the copy, structure, CTA, and visual direction if needed.
> 6. Tell me whether the page matches my offer and the audience I am trying to convert.
> 7. If you see obvious disconnects between the page and my funnel, point them out.
> 8. Prioritize the changes from most important to least important.
> 9. Make the suggestions practical enough that I could actually implement them.
> 10. Run the final user-facing recommendations through my voice profile before you show me the result.
>
> If you need more context about my business, ask me a short follow-up question first.

**Standing correction to rule 9 / item 10 in all three:** these say "Speak Human profile."
