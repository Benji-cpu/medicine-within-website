The Roots

The Lineage Behind the Temple

Temple arts arise from ancient tantrik, priestess, and shamanic lineages, traditions that understood transformation as embodied initiation. People have always gathered like this: in ritual, in rhythm, in candlelight, in honest embodied presence. Before self-help, before workshops, before modern tantra… There were temples.

  • The body as lived prayer
  • Ritual as a language of truth
  • Eros as healing and sacred
  • Community as initiation
  • Presence as medicine
  • Spirit moving through the body

Tantrik Temples are a modern rebirth of these lineages, rooted in sovereignty, relational intelligence, trauma-awareness, and your actual lived humanity.

What happens after the night ends

The temple closes at half past ten. And then there is Wednesday. For most people the night has quietly become a nice memory of something they did, instead of a change in how they live.

Someone said this to me in a conversation, word for word: "We do not do anything with what we learned in previous workshops." The temple did its work. What was missing was somewhere to put it afterwards.

The Living Temple™ is that somewhere. An ongoing room with the same people: one call and one gathering a month, a women's circle, private micro-temples that are never listed publicly. It is the in-between, where the sacredness comes home with you and gets lived for real on an ordinary Tuesday, in your kitchen, with the people who were on the floor beside you.

So the path runs simply. The workshop opens the day. The temple opens the night. The Living Temple™ holds the months between them. You need one temple night with me before you can join, so tonight is where it would begin.
