# Field Research: Women's Embodiment / Sacred Feminine Retreat Market
> Compiled: 2026-08-03
> Subject offer: Sacred Feminine Embodiment Retreat, 4 days, women only, residential, Meeuwenveen (Havelte, Drenthe NL), 8-11 October 2026, €1,222 standard / €1,111 early bird (sold out)
> Method: live web research, August 2026. Every price and claim carries a URL. Anything not directly verified is marked `UNVERIFIED`.
> Companion document: `.masterminds-context/brand-brain.md`. This file supplies the outside-in evidence that document was missing.

**Reading key:** `VERIFIED` = read on a live page at the cited URL. `UNVERIFIED` = could not confirm, do not repeat as fact. `DATED` = real but from a past edition, current price may differ.

---

## 0. The four findings that matter most

1. **€1,222 is at market, not above it.** The closest possible comparable is WildTantra's MATKA 1, a 4-day women-only retreat at the same venue, at €975 excluding lodging. Once lodging is added it lands within a few euros of Sandi's price. The price is not the problem.
2. **Sandi runs the strictest refund policy of any Dutch operator found, on the thinnest proof of any operator at her price.** Fully non-refundable with no transfer clause matches only Sofia Sundari and Coherence Retreats, both of whom carry six-figure audiences. Every Dutch operator found offers either a tiered refund or a free reschedule. This is the sharpest single mismatch in the report.
3. **She is behind the field on delivered integration while using integration as her differentiator.** Three direct competitors bundle post-retreat calls, integration sessions or accountability buddies into the price. Her page ends at the closing ceremony.
4. **The price history is a product change, not a price hike, and nothing on the page says so.** Verified: April 2024 was 3 days at Diemen for €555. September 2025 was 3 days at Diemen for €680. October 2026 is 4 days residential in Drenthe at €1,222. That is a different product. The buyer cannot tell.

### Verdict

**€1,222 sits at the median of the whole European set and comfortably inside the defensible band. It is not overpriced.** At €305 per person per day she is above the Dutch soft-feminine floor (€211-232), below the Dutch ceremonial ceiling (€437-583), and almost exactly level with the German 4-day equivalent (€312) and the Portuguese 6-day premium retreats (€298-320).

**But there is a structural problem the number hides.** The Dutch market has a visible gap:

- **Tier A, soft women's retreat**: €695-845 for 3-4 days, fully catered, private ensuite, max 11.
