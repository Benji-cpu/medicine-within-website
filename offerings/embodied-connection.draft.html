The Longing

Real connection. The kind that actually lands

Life is busy. It moves fast. And somewhere in the middle of all of it, most of us are longing for something we cannot quite name. Stimulation is everywhere, and easy. This is the other thing.

I hear it after temple. On the floor, in the doorway, in the voice notes that arrive on a Wednesday. Thousands of conversations now, and the word that comes up more than tantra, more than intimacy, more than any practice, is myself. I want to feel like myself again. I want to meet people as myself.

Someone said it to me almost exactly like this: “Life is busy and it moves so fast. I am longing to be in the moment. To feel deeply and make actual connections instead of brief small talk.”

That longing does not live in a workshop. It lives on a Tuesday. At a dinner table. Lying next to someone you love and feeling a room away. It is the ordinary ache of a full life that never once asks you to be fully in it.

So this is two hours of practising the thing nobody ever taught you. Coming back into the body. Meeting yourself there. Meeting others from that place. And together, meeting Life.

Call it Life, call it God, call it spirit, call it whatever still makes you ache when you say it out loud. Met more fully. Met more deeply. That is the whole art.

Something real forms, every time

Presence deepens. Trust forms. And somewhere in the second hour, without anyone deciding it, two people who were strangers at half past two are laughing into each other’s shoulder.

The Field

The room that invites all of you

Most rooms ask for a version of you. The friendly one. The capable one. The one that is fine. Holding that version up all evening is quietly exhausting, and it is why you can leave a full room still hungry.

This is a permission field. A room where more of you gets to come alive, in front of other people, in the middle of an ordinary week. You arrive as you are, and you get met there.

The Art of Embodied Connection™ is a monthly workshop in tantra, authentic connection and embodied community, before every Tantrik Temple night. Two hours, 14:30 to 16:30 CET, in the light still up. It stands whole on its own, and it is the opening chapter of the full evening for those who continue into the temple.

We settle first. Breath, body, nervous system. Then we meet: through authentic relating, conscious movement, partner practices and play. Presence deepens. Trust builds. So does real connection. Something real arrives in that room every single time. It always does.

Every practice is an invitation. Nothing is forced. Nothing is expected. Everything is optional. Your yes and your no are both honoured. That is exactly what makes it safe enough to bring the whole of you.

And it is fun. Genuinely, properly fun. There is more laughter in this room than anybody expects, and a kind of sacred silliness that only arrives once everyone stops trying to get it right.

  • Presence. What it feels like in the body to actually arrive, and how to find your way back when you leave.
  • Boundaries that are alive. A yes and a no you can feel in your body before you speak them.
  • Meeting another person. Eye contact, breath, the moment before touch, and what happens when both of you are actually there.
  • Desire without a goal. The practice most people have never been given: wanting something without needing it to arrive somewhere.
  • Belonging. Walking in as a stranger and walking out as part of something. This is the part people are actually here for.

The body becomes a doorway. Connection becomes a practice.
