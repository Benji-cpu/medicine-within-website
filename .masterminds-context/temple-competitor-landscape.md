## Market structure

**Who actually runs monthly mixed temple evenings in the Amsterdam area right now: three players.**
1. Medicine Within (Diemen/Amsterdam, €98-147)
2. Ecstatic Temple (Amsterdam, €89-151, plus satellites; the volume leader with a 150-cap supperclub and a beginner-to-advanced ladder)
3. House of Royal Hearts (Weesp, €75.80-85.80, soft format)

Everyone else is seasonal (Leila ~quarterly, Elizabeth Hunter ~6-weekly full-day), pivoting upmarket (Maia to €334 immersions and international retreats), or apparently exiting (SoulSeductress has nothing published after Nov 7; the "stepping out" story is Sandi's intel, UNVERIFIED on any public page, but the empty calendar after Nov 7 is consistent with it).

**Where the market is thinning:** the €90-130 mixed evening-temple middle. Maia has left that lane (her site now leads with Bali/Belgium/Montreal retreats and a €334 immersion; her Amsterdam evening temples Freya/Lilith/Isis have no next editions listed). SoulSeductress occupied the playful low end (€70-99) and stops publishing after Nov 7. Paradigm Xchange, the microdosing-event leader, currently lists zero upcoming events.

**Price floor/ceiling observed (single ticket, evening temple):** floor €69.80 (SoulSeductress non-gender-conforming tier), cluster €88-128 (Leila, Golden Goddess, Ludamus, Erotic Prayer, Ecstatic Temple women's tiers), ceiling €147-155 (Sandi Late Bird €147, Sophie's Royal Abundant Bird €155, Dancers in Tantra men €147, Ecstatic Temple men €151). Nobody charges more than ~€155 for a single evening; above that the market switches to multi-day formats (€188-334).

## Where Medicine Within sits

- **Price:** her EB €98 lands mid-market; her LB €147 is at the very top of the single-evening range, matched only by men's tiers elsewhere and Sophie's top tier. She is the only organizer whose BASE evening price starts at €98 without a cheaper femme tier. Competitors almost all price women €10-40 below men; she prices flat. That makes her look expensive to women (the majority of most temple audiences) and cheap-ish to men, worth a conscious decision rather than an accident.
- **Frequency:** monthly puts her in the top three, and the only monthly one that is explicitly tantrik-temple-first (Ecstatic Temple is a sexuality-events business, HRH is a celebration night). If SoulSeductress exits and Maia stays in immersion-land, Sandi is plausibly the only monthly mixed temple in Amsterdam proper with a ritual/medicine identity.
- **Differentiation she already owns:** (1) the microdosed tantrik temple: nobody else currently combines microdosing with a temple night (Leila's microdosing parties are parties; SoulSeductress's "microdosed temple" events are historical), and Paradigm Xchange's pause leaves that audience unserved right now; (2) The Art of Embodied Connection workshop+temple same-day bundle, which only Ecstatic Temple loosely mirrors (a €179.80 two-event weekend bundle); (3) The Living Temple membership: no competitor shows any membership/continuity product on Hipsy at all. That is a genuinely open lane.
- **Gaps/opportunities:** no beginner on-ramp product (Ecstatic Temple's "My First Temple" and application-free €75-89 entry points harvest newcomers; Maia explicitly refuses newbies, HRH takes them; Sandi's €98 entry with ritual framing sits between); no couples/duo ticket visible on her temple while nearly every competitor sells duo tiers (€111-160 duos are standard and lift order value); and a visibility question: her Sep 20 temple did NOT appear in the first page of Hipsy's Tempel category (22 events shown of 92) while Ecstatic Temple, SoulSeductress, Sophie, Ludamus, Lexmy and Dancers in Tantra all did. #### Six patterns that fall out of that table

1. **Only one real monthly membership exists in this scene, and it is a venue discount card, not a container.** Temple of Happiness sells **€30/month or €299/year** for 20% off everything plus their weekly Monday Night at €10 instead of €22. That model only works because they have a **weekly** event to discount — a member breaks even at roughly two or three Mondays a month. It is a "come more often" club, not a "get more of me" club. Everyone else avoids recurring billing entirely: Tantric Dance NL sells a €150.80 ten-punch card, The Conscious Club sells €69.95 for five and frames it as *"without the pressure of having to over-commit or sign up,"* and 5Ddating **abolished its monthly fee** on 1 Aug 2025 in favour of a one-time €25/€125 activation. **Prepaid blocks are the norm; subscriptions are the exception and require weekly frequency to make sense.**
2. **The one true subscription found in the whole scene is sold as a plain Hipsy ticket.** Sepehr Maghsoudi's *"Commit to the circle — 2× per month, 24 sessions in 12 months, €408.88"* is a €17-a-session annual membership with no membership platform, no Stripe subscription, no app. Living Temple does not need infrastructure. It needs a ticket type.
3. **Sliding scale by income is standard and uncontroversial in NL group work.** Reuzehulp's Budget €320.80 / Standaard €400.80 / Support €480.80 is a values-aligned way to *raise* the top price rather than lower it. That is a better frame than "founding member" pricing, which expires.
4. **Multi-session containers are where the money actually is.** €400–800 per person for 6–10 evenings, 10 people, no venue production. Denise's €749.80 × 10 men, sold out. Meanwhile a whole evening temple at €98–147 needs 5–8× the bodies to match it.
5. **Potluck is the free dinner.** Lexmy puts a potluck inside her €111.80–139.80 temple day. Tantric Dance NL puts a potluck inside a €20.80 community night. "Dinner included" costs zero and changes the format from event to gathering.
6. **Second-half and duo tickets are free money and Sandi has neither.** EDC sells party-only at €30.80 on a room already paid for. Deep Dating sells a €102.80 duo. Mindful Dating sells "bring a friend". Paradigm Xchange sells "Bring 1 Friend for 2 — €100". Round 1 already flagged that Medicine Within has no duo ticket; this round shows it is universal.

### (d) Five things Sandi could actually run, ranked by effort-to-money

Ranked by return per hour of new work, not by size of prize.

**1. Add a duo ticket and a second-half ticket to events she is already running.** Effort: about twenty minutes in Hipsy. Money: pure uplift on existing nights. **3. Sell Living Temple as a multi-session Hipsy ticket, priced like a men's circle, on a sliding income scale.** Effort: medium, and mostly already done — the container is designed.
