### SECTION 8 · Why Seasons: the depth arc (the tier story woven into the page; Maha 9 process logic)

**Heading:** Why three months, six months, or a year

Real change moves in seasons, not sessions. Here is what time actually buys you:

**Three months is one season. It's a threshold.** Enough time to take one pattern, the one that's costing you the most, and walk it from recognition to a genuinely different way of moving. It has a beginning, a middle, and a completion. It is real work, and it is the shortest container I trust.

**Six months is two seasons. It's a turning.** Because here's what nobody tells you: around the end of the first season, when the newness wears off, the old way fights back. That dip isn't failure, it's the actual doorway. Becoming more of who you really are doesn't only feel like expansion. Sometimes an old way of living has to die first, and that can leave you vulnerable, confused, contracted, before it leaves you free. Six months means the dip happens inside the container instead of after it. We don't just open the pattern. We stay for what grows in its place.

**Twelve months is the whole wheel. It's a rebuild.** A year means the new way of living gets tested against everything: the work deadline, the family visit, the dark of winter, the anniversary of the thing. Every season of the year meets every season of you. This is the container for people who aren't working on a pattern. They're reorganizing an identity. By the end, you don't have insights. You have a different life, and a body that trusts it.

However long we walk: the first season opens, the second holds the turning, and the year makes it yours.

# PART 3 ⟡ THE TIER-DIFFERENTIATION STORY (internal, for reuse everywhere)

The one-breath version, for emails, DMs, and the discovery call. This is the argument for why 6 and 12 exist, and it is a depth arc, not a quantity pitch:

**3 months = one season = a threshold.** One pattern, recognition to completion. The unit of real change.

**6 months = two seasons = the turning.** The predictable dip around month three, when the old way fights back, happens INSIDE the container instead of after it ends. Season one opens the pattern; season two grows what replaces it. You're not buying more time, you're buying the part of the process where most people relapse, held.

**12 months = the whole wheel = a rebuild.** The new way gets tested against every season of an actual life: deadlines, family, winter, anniversaries. Identity-level work needs a full year of reps because life doesn't repeat its tests in ninety days. And the pricing tells the same story: the year costs three seasons, you live four.

Never sell the tiers as "more sessions." Sell them as which part of the arc gets held: the opening (3), the turning (6), or the becoming-ordinary (12). The deepest promise of the year is her own line: transformation is living differently, not remembering an experience.

# PART 4 ⟡ APPLICATION QUESTIONS (Direct/Piercing register)

This is a safe space, and I'd rather have your truth than your reach.
