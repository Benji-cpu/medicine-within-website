## THE CORE THESIS: invert the business

She runs the lowest-margin product in her catalogue as her primary income, with
nothing behind it.

**One temple:** ~35 people x €110 = €3,850 gross. Minus venue, minus ~€490 of
comped tickets, minus production, DJ, support team, and 3-8 weeks of promo
labour. One day of delivery. Monthly. This is why she is exhausted.

**One container:** 10 people x ~€700 = €7,000. Six evenings over three months,
~12 delivery hours. No venue, no mattresses, no gender balancing, no production,
no ticket anxiety, money collected before it starts. Denise Kooij sells exactly
this out, twice a year.

**Same money, one fifth the machinery.**

→ **Temples should become the funnel. Containers should become the revenue.**
This is not a pivot away from temples. It is finally putting something behind
them. She has 124 repeat buyers producing 54% of revenue who keep re-buying the
SAME night because nothing deeper exists to buy. Every month she fills a room of
35 people at peak openness and sells them nothing.

## THE THREE CONTAINERS SHE CAN ALREADY RUN

1. **Men's container. The sharpest opportunity found in the entire research.**
   Her rooms are ~2/3 male; men email her about gender balance constantly;
   nobody else in her scene serves men except Denise, who runs hers *held by
   women*, the exact configuration Sandi is built for. 6 evenings, 10 men,
   ~€7,000/cohort, 2x/year. Fixes the gender ratio from the supply side instead
   of comping tickets. Proposed: €699 early / €749 / €799.
2. **Women's container.** She already owns the IP (Elements & Archetypes,
   recyclable) and it is closest to her soul. It is also the paid rung her
   women's line has never had: 104 women entered via free circles, 4 ever bought
   again. Same shape, €650-800.
3. **The Living Temple.** The connective tissue, already designed and priced
   (€169 founding season, then €69/mo).

## WHAT THE TEMPLE BECOMES
The best top-of-funnel in Amsterdam: her range, her DJ, her production, her
reputation. Optimised for reach and memory, not for squeezing five more tickets.
This frees her to reinvest in it (dance back in, Leila co-facilitating Dec 20,
better production, venue upgrade) precisely because it no longer carries income
alone.

## HOW SHE OWNS THE MARKET
Not on volume (Ecstatic Temple wins). Not on rating (the field sits at 4.7+).
**Own depth and continuity.** They sell nights; she sells the year. Range gets
people in, containers hold them, the retreat is the apex. It is the only
position nobody occupies, and her own repeat-buyer data says her audience
already wants it.
Message line: **"Everywhere else you have to choose. Here you don't."**
