Subject: a two-minute polarity check-in

Beautiful you,

A different kind of practice today. Not breath this time, but something just as small, just as real.

I keep wanting to hand you pieces of this work before the weekend, not because I have to, but because I want your body to feel a little of this before you ever walk into the room.

Inside Embodied Tantra Weekend Immersion, one of the pillars we explore is polarity: the dance of masculine and feminine energy that lives inside every person, regardless of gender. Directing and receiving. Structure and flow. Initiating and allowing.

Most of us are far more fluent in one than the other.

Here is the check-in. Two minutes. Nothing needed but a quiet moment.

Think back over today. Find one moment you were directing: choosing, initiating, making something happen.

Now find one moment you were receiving: letting something come to you, staying open, doing nothing but allowing yourself to be met.

Notice which one your body found easy.

Notice which one made you hesitate, even slightly, even for a second.

That hesitation is just the muscle that has gone a little quiet from disuse.

One woman from a past Embodied Tantra weekend, Stef, said it better than I can:

"I'm so happy about everything there is to know and experience about polarity and the masculine and feminine! It puts so much in perspective and in place for me... Now I can start to play: be aware, use both sides effectively and trust my intuition on what feels good."

That is what this pillar of the work gives you. Fluency in a language your body already speaks, half of which has just gone quiet.

We go so much deeper into this over the weekend. And on the Sunday, Deeper Intimacy lets polarity move into real relational space, where it gets to be met by another person, not just noticed alone.

If two minutes of noticing landed something in you, wait until you feel it in a room full of people doing the same work.
