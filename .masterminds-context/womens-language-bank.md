### Gender-confidence method
- **WOMAN-CONFIRMED** — a women-only container by design (Temple of Fire: form asks "tell one other woman… the woman who just wrote all of this"), **or** self-identification in her own text ("my husband", "great strength of women", "my future partner/father of my future children", "in my feminine").
- **LIKELY-WOMAN** — unambiguous given name + no contradicting text. Used for most of C and D.
- **UNKNOWN** — anonymised quote dumps (E, F), or names that don't resolve (e.g. "Tiny Kanters", "Cal Amir", "Dyor wells").
- **MAN** — appendix only.

Ticket type is **not** a gender signal. Three men in dataset C bought "Individual Shakti" tickets (Raf Frateur, Jos Santen — "I am a single man" — and Roel van Reenen). Never infer gender from Shakti/Shiva.
