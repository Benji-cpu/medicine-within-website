Before Your First Event

What to Expect if This Is Your First Time

Almost everyone is nervous before their first event. A plunge into the unknown, and a strong desire to find peace in the mystery anyway. If you're feeling that reading this, you are in good company. Most of the room felt it too.

Nothing is forced. Nothing is expected. Everything is optional. Your yes and your no are both honoured. Every practice is an invitation, and observing is a full way of being in the room. First-timers arrive constantly, solo, coupled, in open relationships, complete beginners to tantra and Kambô alike. You do not need experience. You do not need a partner. You need willingness.
