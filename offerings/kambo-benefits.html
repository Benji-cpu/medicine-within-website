Kambô is an ancient Amazonian purification medicine, worked with for what it may support physically, emotionally, and energetically. It is non-psychedelic, deeply somatic, and works through the body’s own intelligence. What follows is what people commonly report, held with honesty rather than promise.

The Spirit of Kambô

Why we sit with the frog

Physical

Traditionally worked with for physical cleansing. Many people describe a lightness in the body afterwards, and a clearing of what felt stuck.

Emotional

Releasing 'Panema', the indigenous term for bad luck, heavy energy, or emotional stagnation. What is released wants to be met.

Spiritual

Restoring a sense of inner authority, sharpening intuition, and clearing the path back to what is yours to do.
