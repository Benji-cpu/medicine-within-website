## MAIA, firsthand review from Sacred Space (WhatsApp VN, 2026-08-27)
Sandi asked him directly (she joined something of Maia's years ago, "was not
impressed," and has heard Maia described as an unsafe facilitator). His answer,
having attended one of Maia's spaces recently:

**Her format is the opposite of volume.** "In some spaces you go and there's 20,
30, 40 people and you meet half the group, lots of small interactions. Hers felt
more like three, four, five really deep, really intense practices, and each time
you go with one or two people. It felt more like a retreat in that sense."
→ FEWER, DEEPER practices. This is why 50 people at her events (if true) is not
the same product as 50 at a connection-heavy temple.

**She over-warns on purpose.** "She has like a million warnings, WhatsApps and
messages... she writes her emails almost in a way that is scary: hey, this is
probably not for you, watch out." Full self-responsibility framing, explicitly
"working intentionally with triggers." He suspects she evolved this after
feedback. His verdict on the unsafe reputation: "if somebody goes to her space
and leaves feeling shaken and complains, it's mainly theirs," because the framing
is so heavy up front.

**Staffing:** six or seven emotional support staff. (Compare: Sandi's comped
women often ARE her support field. Maia staffs it deliberately.)

**Level:** "medium to medium advanced, same as ISTA. I've never yet seen a space
that is advanced to super advanced." And Maia told him **that was one of her
SOFTER spaces.**

**Authenticity read:** "She's not just selling you cliches. She's a bit dark and
wild like herself." He was impressed; he had not been years ago.

### What Sandi can take from this
1. Heavy pre-framing is a safety mechanism AND a marketing filter. Her forms
   already do some of this; the emails do not.
2. Paid support staff instead of comped tickets is a real alternative model.
3. "Advanced to super advanced" is an unclaimed level in this market, per someone
   who attends widely. Deeper Intimacy is her closest product to that lane.
