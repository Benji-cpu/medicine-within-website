A Living Initiation

Four days in the forest at Havelte, where archetypal work, tantra, and ritual meet in one living temple field. You move, you sound, you journey, you rest. By Sunday you are back in the body that already knows who you are.

Small by design, never a crowd. Trauma-aware throughout. Nothing is forced. Nothing is expected. Everything is optional. Your yes and your no are both honoured.

Who Comes Here

You Might Recognise Yourself

This is for the woman who has done the healing work in her head and still feels a long way from her body. Spiritual but craving more eros, or erotic but craving more soul, and she has not yet found the room that holds both at once.

She is often nervous walking in. She comes anyway, because something in her already knows this is the room. The room that invites all of her, and then makes it safe enough to bring.
